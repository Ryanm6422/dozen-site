About Dozen Ultimate

Building Champions in College Station Since 1998

A Legacy of Competition and Camaraderie

Texas A&M "Dozen Ultimate" is a premier collegiate club program competing at the Division-I level in the USA Ultimate (USAU) South Central Region.

Origin of the Name: Inspired by Texas A&M’s world-famous "12th Man" tradition, the program adopted the moniker "Dozen" to represent unified strength, selfless service, and the relentless Aggie spirit brought onto the field every point.

Representing Texas A&M University with pride, Dozen operates as a single unified team competing for USAU College Series bids while welcoming players of all skill levels and developing recruits into elite handlers, cutters, and defenders.

Program History & Excellence

Over Two Decades of Aggie Ultimate Championship Tradition

1998

Program Founded

Texas A&M Men's Ultimate was officially recognized as a Sport Club, establishing "Dozen" in honor of the Aggie 12th Man tradition.

2001

First USAU Nationals Appearance

Dozen made its inaugural debut at the USA Ultimate D-I College National Championships, putting Texas A&M on the national ultimate radar.

2014 – 2017

4-Year Consecutive Nationals Dynasty

Dozen established a dominant era, qualifying for four consecutive USAU College National Championships (2014, 2015, 2016, and 2017) as a premier South Central power.

2026 – 2027

Building the Next Era

Focused on growing new talent, relentless work ethic, and building a strong team culture as Dozen gears up for another competitive season of Aggie Ultimate.
